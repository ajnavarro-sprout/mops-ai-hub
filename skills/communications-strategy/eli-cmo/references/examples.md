# Calibration examples

These are the examples ELI-CMO was designed against. Use them to calibrate tone
and level of translation, not as templates to copy verbatim.

## Narrative-style translations

**Geek speak:** Rebuilt the decision logic in LeanData so that leads can be
routed to the right inbound and outbound BDR using a standard queue.

**C-suite:** Improved conversion of MQL to meetings by routing MQLs to the
right BDR with corresponding SLAs, at the right time, with relevant context.

---

**Geek speak:** Ran 70 webinars that resulted in 457 MQLs and 119 booked
meetings.

**C-suite:** Demo Lab webinars had fewer registrations compared to TOFU
webinars, but had a higher conversion to meetings. Recommendation to expand
Demo Labs to more product and solution areas.

*(Note what happened here: the raw totals disappeared entirely. What survived
was a comparison between two programs and a recommendation. This is the
"deliver insight and a recommended action" and "drive more/less/different"
principles in action.)*

---

**Geek speak:** Marketing sourced revenue grew 41% YoY while Program Spend
grew 13% YoY.

**C-suite:** Every $1 spent on programs resulted in $10 in MSB in FY23 vs. $8
in MSB in FY22.

*(Two growth rates became one ratio -- return per dollar spent, compared
across years. This is easier for an exec to act on than two independent growth
percentages.)*

---

**Geek speak:** Assign differentiating priority values (P1, P2, P3, P4) to
MQLs to trigger or bypass the account predictive model so we can assign the
corresponding SLA.

**C-suite:** Differentiate MQLs to drive urgency to "hot" leads with faster
<4 hr follow-up SLA.
